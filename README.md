# PIP Evidence Builder™

> **Track symptoms. Record daily challenges. Organise medical evidence. Prepare for PIP assessments.**
>
> Built by [YourUKHelpDesk](https://github.com/MyGreathouse) · Author: Alexander Hart

---

## 🔒 Your data never leaves your device

Everything you enter is saved in your **browser's own localStorage** — on your device only. There is no server, no account, no cloud sync. The app works fully offline once installed.

## 📱 Install as an app (PWA)

### iPhone / iPad
1. Open the live link above in **Safari**
2. Tap the **Share** icon → **Add to Home Screen**
3. The app appears on your home screen — works offline

### Android
1. Open the live link in **Chrome**
2. Tap **⋮ menu** → **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**

### Desktop (Chrome / Edge)
1. Open the live link
2. Click the **install icon** in the address bar (or the banner that appears)
3. The app opens in its own window with no browser chrome

## 📋 What's inside

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| Personal profile | Claimant details, GP, specialists |
| Health conditions | Unlimited condition register |
| Symptom tracker | Daily log with severity scale |
| Daily living diary | All 9 PIP activity areas with inline status selectors |
| Mobility tracker | Distance, pain, fatigue, falls, worst-day flagging |
| Medication tracker | Full medication list |
| Appointments | Complete appointment history |
| Evidence vault | Document index with gap analysis |
| Real-life examples | Dated incident records |
| Assessment prep | Guided question prompts |
| Readiness scores | Preparation score + Evidence strength score |
| Evidence pack | Full printable report across all sections |

---

## 🖨 Print / export

- **Print evidence pack** — opens a formatted multi-page report, save as PDF from your browser
- **Back up data** — downloads all your entries as a `.json` file to your device

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

PIP Evidence Builder™ is an evidence organisation and record-keeping tool only. It does not provide legal or welfare rights advice, predict award outcomes, or determine eligibility for PIP or any other benefit.
